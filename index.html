Supplement Timing: Morning or Night?

Many supplement labels say nothing about timing, and the ones that do rarely explain why. The reason timing matters at all is absorption: some ingredients absorb better with food, others on an empty stomach, and a few are best taken at a consistent time each day simply so the habit sticks. A label that ignores timing is not necessarily wrong, but a label that explains it is giving you useful information about how the product was designed.

Morning timing suits products tied to daytime routines, because they are easier to remember alongside breakfast and because any stimulating ingredients are kept away from bedtime. Night timing suits products whose ingredients are meant to be processed overnight, or that are simply easier to tolerate with an evening meal. Neither is universally better; the right answer depends on the ingredient, the form and your own routine.

What matters more than the hour is consistency. Taking a product at roughly the same time each day keeps intake steady, which is what most testing assumes. Skipping days and doubling up later is a worse pattern than any choice of morning or night.
